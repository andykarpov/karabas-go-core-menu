# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Overview

The boot menu core of the Karabas Go, Mini and Mini G boards: Xilinx Spartan-6 XC6SLX25, ISE 14.7 (XST). The core draws its own menu (`docs/PLAN.md`): a VexRiscv rv32im soft CPU (`menu_soc`) draws the core list and the Setup pages in the look of the old FT812 menu, the same on all three boards. The firmware has no boot menu any more (Ф4): it loads the core and its ROMs and serves the CPU over link v2 RPC. Three tops: `src/rtl/karabas_go_top.v` (VGA through the video DAC, the FT812 video on it too), `karabas_mini_top.v` and `karabas_minig_top.v` (HDMI; no FT812 video since the video modes: the firmware does not start it for the boot core). The defines HW_ID1 (Go), HW_ID2 (Mini), HW_ID2 + HW_ID3 (Mini G) are set in the `.xst` files.

- The CPU: `src/rtl/soc/menu_soc.v` (VexRiscv of karabas-go-core-doom, I$ / D$ 4K, `rtl/soc/vexriscv/VexRiscv.v`) on clk_cpu 75 MHz. Its program `src/roms/menu.bin` (built from `src/sw`) is the ROM of `boot.kg` (`core.json` `roms`: address 0); the firmware loads it into the SRAM (LOAD kind 0, caps bit 4: the core drives LD_READY; no loading screen for the boot core) and the CPU is held in reset until the load has ended. The code runs from the SRAM behind the caches. The memory map and the MMIO registers are in the header of `menu_soc.v` and in `src/sw/mmio.h`.
- The SRAM (both 2M x 8 chips as one 2M x 16, `menu_sram.v`): the program, the data, two frame buffers of 800 x 600 x 8 bpp at 0x300000 and 0x380000 (a line is 800 bytes in both video modes). `menu_sram` clears all of it after the PLL lock and again before a core starts: the CPU writes the `CORES LAUNCH` request and the MMIO LAUNCH register, the CPU stops for good, the SRAM is filled with zeros (84 ms), then the request goes to the firmware. Every core starts with clean memory. `sram_clear.v` and `vga_sync.v` are still in the tree but not in the build.
- The video: two VESA modes, the MMIO VMODE register, `[ft812] video_mode` of karabas.ini (0 640 x 480 at 25 MHz, the default and the mode after the start; 1 800 x 600 at 40 MHz; Setup > Display > Video mode tries a mode with a 15 s Keep / Back dialog). The pixel clock `clk_pix` is a BUFGMUX of the two PLL outputs. `rtl/vga/menu_video.v` scans the shown frame buffer out (the syncs negative at the pins in 640 x 480, positive in 800 x 600; the tops invert them by `mode_o`), a palette bank per buffer (a picture is drawn into the hidden buffer with its own colours, then shown); `menu_compose.v` blends it (alpha 0..15) over the FT812-style gradient (the `[ft812]` colours of karabas.ini, written by the CPU); until the CPU shows its first frame the background is black (`menu_bg.v`, the glow of the old menu, is still in the tree but not in the build). The bitmap OSD of link v2 (`kg_l2_osd_overlay`, scale 2) is on top of it all (the firmware's messages).
- RPC: `kg_l2_rpc` of modules in `kg_mcu2` (target 08, `ENABLE_RPC`, `RPC_SERVICES` 16'h000F = SYS, CFG, CORES, HID), the mailbox at MMIO 0x80002000 (`src/sw/rpc.c`). The spec is karabas-go-firmware `docs/link_v2.md` 5.9; the design notes and the later services are in `docs/RPC.md`. Until the release the modules and the firmware are on their `feature/rpc` branches.
- The ESP8266 (`rtl/uart/esp8266.vhd`): its uart goes to PORT 1 of `kg_mcu2` (the passthrough to the MCU), except while the CPU has it (MMIO ESP_OWN: Setup > Wi-Fi, NTP sync); then the MCU gets nothing.
- MIDI: `rtl/soc/menu_midi_mux.v` merges the notes of the CPU (MMIO MIDI, the key click) with the thru of a USB MIDI keyboard (PORT 2 of `kg_mcu2`, caps bit 9) at whole messages into `kg_midi_tx` on `MIDI_TX` (31250 baud, the SAM2695).
- Sound: the program plays MIDI only (`src/sw/sound.c`: the key click, the start melody of `[ft812] sound`, docs/PLAN.md "Smaller ROMs"). `rtl/soc/menu_sound.v`, two PCM channels (raw signed 16-bit mono in the SRAM) at 48 kHz, is still in the bitstreams, silent (the PCM sounds were dropped 2026-11-23), added with saturation to the audio: on the Go the PCM5102 gets the menu sound only (no ADC here); on the Mini the sigma-delta DACs and HDMI get it with the ADC (`i2s_transceiver`); on the Mini G the PCM5102 and HDMI with the ADC.
- The SD slot of the FPGA is passed through to the MCU (`kg_mcu2` `ENABLE_SD2`: chip select `MCU_SPI_SD2_CS_N` on the Go, `MCU_IO[2]` on the Mini / Mini G): the firmware shows it to the PC as the second USB drive (`msc2`, BTN2 held 2 s, `buttons` of the json). SD_CLK: on the Go `SD2_SCK` of `kg_mcu2`, on the Mini the `MCU_SCK` pad (local routing, not a clock pin before rev G), on the Mini G the IBUFG output of MCU_SCK, as the FT812 SCK (a BUFG cannot drive a pad).

The board modules (MCU link, memories, clocks, DACs, OSD, HDMI) come from the git submodule `modules/` ([karabas-go-modules](https://github.com/andykarpov/karabas-go-modules), shared by the Karabas Go cores; clone with `--recursive` or run `git submodule update --init`). A change to them is made there and taken here by updating the submodule commit. Its `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/PLAN.md` have the rules (Verilog-2001 for XST, tests, checks of LUTs and timing).

This core is the loader of every other core: a broken build leaves the board without a menu. Test in simulation and on the hardware of the three boards before a release. Test builds are `boot.kg` itself (user rule since 2026-11-23: the self-drawing menu is the boot core on the boards; `menutest.kg` is not built any more). A change of the CPU program only (`src/sw`) needs no ISE run: the committed bitstreams are repacked with the new `roms/menu.bin` (`python3 make_core.py core.json boot.kg`, the `build` of the json bumped).

## Build

- `cd src && make` builds the three boards (`make go`, `mini`, `minig` one of them; `make minig-xst`, `-impl`, `-core` one step; `make minig-report` the utilization and timing of the last build). It runs the ISE command-line flow and sources `/opt/Xilinx/14.7/ISE_DS/settings64.sh` (`ISE_SETTINGS=` to change it). The boards share `xst/` and `_ngo/` in `src/`, so they are built one after the other; a board takes a few minutes.
- Inputs per board: `karabas_<board>.prj` (the source list, the shared modules as `"../modules/rtl/..."`), `.xst`, `.ucf`, `.ut`. There is no ISE GUI project (`.xise`); the `karabas_*_top.*` files in `src/` are leftovers of it, not used. The Makefile has the map / par options (those of tsconf). The map cost table is per board, `MAP_T_<board>` (the Go 1, the Mini 2, the Mini G 3): when a build misses a constraint by a placement (a path without logic and a long route), try another (2...5) before changing the design.
- `core` packs `karabas_<board>.bin` with `core.json` (one file for all boards: the common keys, `boards` for what differs; kgo2, karabas-go-tools `docs/kgo2.md`) and the ROM `roms/menu.bin` into `boot.kg` (`make <board>-core`: `make_core.py core.json boot.kg --boards <board>` puts that board's bitstream in) with `make_core.py`. The stamp depends on the `.bin` and `core.json` only: a new `menu.bin` alone does not repack `boot.kg`. The `.bin`, `.kg` and `roms/*.bin` files are committed as the release; `build` of the json is `date +%y%m%d%H` of the release build.
- The CPU program: `make -C src/sw` (xPack `riscv-none-elf-gcc`, `XPACK=` the bin directory, default `~/opt/xpack-riscv-none-elf-gcc-15.2.0-1/bin`; `-march=rv32im`, newlib-nano, `link.ld`) writes `src/roms/menu.bin`. `make fonts` / `images` regenerate the committed `fonts_gen.h` (Noto Sans, 4 bpp: f27 all the text, f30 the clock digits, f20 the Gamepad labels; 8-byte glyphs by code ranges) and `images_gen.h` (from `sw/assets/ft_bitmaps.h`, the pictures the firmware had; the character in runs).
- The `osd` items of the json (Reset, Global Reset) are left from the old core: the tops leave `SW` of `kg_mcu2` open.

## Timing

- Clocks (the tops), one PLL from CLK_50MHZ (VCO 600 MHz): clk_cpu 75 MHz (/8, out 2: the CPU, the SRAM, `kg_mcu2`, the ESP and MIDI uarts, `menu_sound`), clk_sys 40 MHz (/15, out 0: the audio and DACs, the ADC, the HDMI audio samples and reset; the pixel clock of 800 x 600), 25 MHz (/24: the pixel clock of 640 x 480; out 3 on the Go and the Mini G, out 4 on the Mini), on the Mini / Mini G clk_8 8 MHz (/75, out 1: FT_CLK_OUT), MIDI_CLK 12.24 MHz (/49) on out 1 on the Go and on out 3 on the Mini. `clk_pix` = BUFGMUX(25, 40 MHz), its select `pix_sel` = VMODE through a clk_sys flip-flop: the scanout of `menu_video`, `menu_compose`, the OSD overlay, the VGA pins of the Go, HDMI. The PLL outputs of 40 and 25 MHz have no BUFG in `kg_pll`: the BUFG of clk_sys and the BUFGMUX are in the top.
- HDMI (`rtl/hdmi`): `hdmi_top.v` (the video in clk_pix, the audio samples of clk_sys passed with a toggle, the reset) around zhdmi (`hdmi_tx` of modules, `INPUT_REG(1)`, `OUTPUT_REG(1)`: the encoder -> serializer path); `hdmi_pll.v` is a DCM_SP of 5 x clk_pix (125 / 200 MHz), reset on a mode change. The FT812 path (DCM_CLKGEN from the measured FT_CLK, `freq_counter.vhd`, the FT fifo) was removed with the video modes: it needed a third input of the pixel clock BUFGMUX.
- The three ucf have the same timing block (the end of each file, then the parts of the board): PERIOD on CLK_50MHZ (derived onto the PLL outputs), the groups TG_CLK_SYS on the BUFG `clk_sys`, TG_CLK_CPU on `pll_clk<2>`, TG_CLK_PIX on `clk_pix`; TS_PIX (25 ns inside the pixel clock: it carries the derived PERIODs of both BUFGMUX inputs, the 40 MHz bound covers 25 MHz) and a TIG on the KEEP net `pix_sel`; TS_CPU_TO_SYS / _SYS_TO_CPU / _CPU_TO_PIX / _PIX_TO_CPU (13.3 ns DATAPATHONLY: no common period, the paths are toggles, the line buffer and palette RAMs and settings held stable), TS_SYS_TO_PIX / _PIX_TO_SYS (25 ns DATAPATHONLY), TS_SRAM_OUT / TS_SRAM_IN (8 / 4 ns, the IOB flip-flops of `menu_sram`, a two-clock read of the 10 ns SRAM), TS_CLK_MCU 32 MHz, DATAPATHONLY bounds for MCU_SCK <-> clk_sys / clk_cpu; on the Mini / Mini G TS_SER_D (the TMDS words -> the serializers). On the Go the VGA outputs are IOB registers bounded by TS_VGA_OUT (V_CLK is ~clk_pix). Each exception is explained there; do not TIG paths that are real.
- MCU_SCK of the Mini G goes through IBUFG + BUFG; the SCK of the FT812 SPI and SD_CLK take the IBUFG output.
- No clocks from logic and no latches: check "Clock Information" of the `.syr`.

## Simulation

- `src/sim/soc/run_soc_sim.sh` (Verilator): `tb_menu_soc`, `menu_soc` + `menu_video` + `menu_compose` + `kg_l2_rpc` with the real program against a model of the MCU (the core list over RPC, keys, LAUNCH) and of the ESP; runs (640 x 480): keys, mouse, sound (the first notes of the start melody on MIDI), Setup, Wi-Fi, and Setup in 800 x 600 (`TB_LOOK=video_mode=1`), each prints `tb_menu_soc: 0 errors` and writes a frame (`*.ppm`). It runs `make -C ../../sw` first, which rewrites `src/roms/menu.bin`.
- `src/sim/midi/run_midi_sim.sh` (iverilog): `tb_midi_mux`, the keyboard and CPU streams of `menu_midi_mux`; prints `tb_midi_mux: 0 errors`.
- `src/sw/host/run.sh` (gcc on the host): the drawing code of the menu with a fake firmware (`host/host.c`), the frames of the screens as PNG in `src/sw/host/out/640` and `/800` (both video modes). For quick looks at a screen; the UI needs the user's approval on frames.
- `src/sim/run_hdmi_sim.sh` (ISim): `tb_hdmi_top`, the menu video through the BUFGMUX of 25 / 40 MHz and `hdmi_pll`, switched back and forth, every pixel where it enters `hdmi_tx`, the pixel clock, the audio samples. Prints `tb_hdmi_top: 0 errors`.
- Left from the old menu, they do not test the build: `run_eq_sim.sh` (`kg_osd_overlay` against the old `overlay.vhd`), `run_mcu_sim.sh` (`kg_mcu` link v1 against the old `mcu.vhd`; the tops use `kg_l2_osd_overlay` / `kg_mcu2` now) and `run_sram_sim.sh` (`sram_clear.v`, replaced by the clear of `menu_sram`).
- The tests of the shared modules (`kg_l2_rpc`, `kg_mcu2`) are in `modules/sim` (`run_all.sh`).

## Layout

- `src/rtl/karabas_*_top.v`: the boards: PLL, `kg_mcu2`, `menu_soc`, `menu_video`, `menu_compose`, `kg_l2_osd_overlay`, `esp8266`, `menu_midi_mux`, the DACs, HDMI.
- `src/rtl/soc`: `menu_soc.v` (CPU, MMIO, loader, LAUNCH), `menu_sram.v` (SRAM, the clear), `menu_sound.v`, `menu_midi_mux.v`, `vexriscv/` (the generated VexRiscv and its config). `src/rtl/vga`: `menu_video.v`, `menu_compose.v` (`menu_bg.v` not in the build). `src/rtl/hdmi`: `hdmi_top.v`, `hdmi_pll.v`. `src/rtl/uart`: `esp8266.vhd`, `uart_rx.vhd`, `uart_tx.vhd`. `src/rtl/adc`: `i2s_transceiver.vhd` (the ADC of the Mini / Mini G).
- `src/sw`: the CPU program: `main.c` (the loop), `browser.c` (the core list, autoload), `setup.c` / `settings.c` (Setup and the karabas.ini settings over RPC CFG), `wifi.c` / `wifi_page.c` / `esp.c` / `esp_update.c` / `ntp.c` (Wi-Fi and NTP over the ESP uart), `pad_page.c` (Gamepad, HID learning over RPC), `ui.c` / `gfx.c` / `fonts.c` (drawing), `sound.c`, `rpc.c`, `crt0.S`, `link.ld`, `syscalls.c`; `lib/` files of the firmware `lib/common` used as they are (a change goes to the firmware first); `assets/`, `tools/` (the generators), `host/`.
- `src/roms`: `menu.bin` (the ROM of `boot.kg`).
- `src/sim/ref`: the old code (new_hdmi_pll d31dec0) of the old equivalence tests.
- `docs/PLAN.md` (goals, decisions, phases, status), `docs/RPC.md`, `docs/img` (the frames compared with the FT812 menu).
